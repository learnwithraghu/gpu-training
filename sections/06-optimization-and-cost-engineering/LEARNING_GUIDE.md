# Section 06 - Optimization and Cost Engineering

## Teaching Outcome

Learners can distinguish compute-bound vs memory-bound workloads and choose cost-aware optimizations.

## Session Plan (75 minutes)

1. Bottleneck classification
2. Precision tradeoff experiment
3. Batch/throughput tuning loop
4. Cost estimate synthesis

## Teaching Notes

- Reinforce that fastest is not always cheapest.
- Ask learners to report both performance and estimated cost.
- Compare at least two optimization strategies before deciding.

## Notebook Cadence (ultra-short typing)

1. Tradeoff prompt (speed vs cost)
2. Micro code cell (2-6 lines)
3. Metric output review
4. Decision checkpoint

Explicitly ask learners to justify a choice after every measured run.
