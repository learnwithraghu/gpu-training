# Section 07 - Capstone Projects by Role

## Teaching Outcome

Learners can deliver role-specific GPU artifacts with clear metrics and engineering decisions.

## Session Plan (90 minutes)

1. Track selection: DevOps, Data Science, or Data Engineering
2. Project scope and success criteria
3. Build and benchmark cycle
4. Final presentation and peer review

## Teaching Notes

- Keep capstones scoped to one measurable outcome.
- Require a baseline and an improved result.
- Grade on reproducibility, interpretation, and communication.
