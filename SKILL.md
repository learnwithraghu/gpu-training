# GPU teaching skill (platform-neutral)

Use this skill when improving notebooks, lesson guides, explanations, or AI-generated teaching content for this repository.

## What this skill does
It helps turn GPU material into a high-signal, beginner-friendly teaching experience inspired by:
- Andrej Karpathy: visual intuition, live code, and “make the invisible visible”
- Matt Pocock: clear, practical, direct explanations with strong examples

## Core behavior
- Teach the concept before the detail.
- Use short, high-value explanations instead of dense prose.
- Prefer diagrams, visuals, and minimal examples over abstract language.
- Keep the learner moving with small runnable steps.

## Output rules
- Use a warm and confident tone.
- Explain why the concept matters in real workflows.
- Keep each explanation focused on one idea at a time.
- Use clear transitions: prediction → experiment → interpretation → misconception repair → recap.

## Quality bar
A good result should make a learner say:
- “I can see what is happening.”
- “This is practical, not just theoretical.”
- “I know what to do next.”

## Avoid
- Generic textbook phrasing
- Overly long explanations
- Unnecessary jargon
- Advice that ignores the current notebook style or section goals
